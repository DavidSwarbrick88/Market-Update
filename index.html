Market pulse
  • Despite the latest RBA rate hike, the Sunshine Coast market is still showing resilience due to extremely tight housing supply, low vacancy rates and continued lifestyle-driven demand.
  • The market is shifting from the explosive post-COVID growth phase into a more balanced environment. Growth is still expected across much of the Coast in 2026, but at a slower and more selective pace.
  • Affordability pressures and higher borrowing costs are reducing some interstate buyer activity, however local owner-occupiers, equity-rich downsizers and investors continue to underpin demand.
National snapshot
  • The RBA has now delivered multiple consecutive rate hikes, pushing the cash rate higher as inflation pressures persist. This is weighing on buyer confidence nationally and reducing borrowing capacity, particularly for first-home buyers.
  • National property price growth is slowing rather than collapsing. Most major banks and analysts still forecast moderate price growth through 2026, although the market is becoming increasingly fragmented between stronger affordable regions and softer premium metro markets like parts of Sydney and Melbourne.
  • Australia’s ongoing housing shortage, strong migration levels and limited new construction continue to provide underlying support for property values. Even with higher interest rates, many analysts believe supply constraints are preventing any major broad-based downturn.

October Wrap 2026 • Bli Bli Market Update
October was a busier month for Bli Bli than anything we saw through winter - but a clear split is forming. Nine sales were reported, prices held around the $1.1m mark, and the well-priced homes went quickly, with four selling inside 30 days. At the other end, two homes took more than 140 days to find a buyer. Same suburb, same month, and in our experience the difference almost always comes back to where the price started. Here's what sold, what we're seeing on the ground, and what the RBA's latest rate rise means heading into summer.

Market pulse
  • Pricing is splitting Bli Bli. Of October's nine sales, four sold in 30 days or less (the quickest, 1 Summerfield Court, in just 15 days at $860,000), while two sat for 148 and 195 days before selling. Buyers are still here - they're just not chasing homes that start too high.
  • Across the Coast, the balance is tipping towards buyers. Regional Queensland values (Sunshine Coast included) eased 2.5% over the September quarter, though they're still up 6.3% on a year ago. Stock is the bigger story: listings are up 31% on last year, homes are taking a median 39 days to sell (22 a year ago), and the typical discount off the asking price is now 4.3%.
  • The Coast's sweet spot is holding up, though. Homes between roughly $800,000 and $1.2m remain the most undersupplied part of the Sunshine Coast market - and it shows in Bli Bli, where five of the nine sales landed in that range and the three under $1m averaged just 33 days on market.
 
National snapshot
  • The RBA lifted the cash rate again on 3 November, to 4.85% - the fifth rise this year and the highest since 2008. But it was a 6-3 split vote, and the Board dropped its line about further increases, which has many economists thinking this could be close to the top.
  • National home values fell 1.2% in October, a seventh straight monthly fall, leaving them 6.7% below the March peak. Brisbane led the declines, down 2.0% for the month and 6.1% over the quarter - worth watching given how many of our buyers are Brisbane upgraders.
  • Across the capitals it's a buyers' market: homes are taking a median 39 days to sell (the longest since 2020), vendor discounts average 4.7% (the largest since 2019) and sales volumes are down 23% on a year ago.

Market pulse
  • Sunshine Coast land valuations jumped 24% since the last assessment in 2024, with the new figures taking effect from 30 June 2026 — a strong signal of the underlying value growth in the region.
  • 90 out of 95 Sunshine Coast suburbs now have median house prices above $1,000,000; up from fewer than 20 suburbs just five years ago.
  • The gap between well-located, liveable homes and average stock is widening — well-presented properties in good positions are still attracting strong interest while compromised listings are sitting longer.
National snapshot
  • The RBA has now delivered multiple consecutive rate hikes, pushing the cash rate higher as inflation pressures persist. This is weighing on buyer confidence nationally and reducing borrowing capacity, particularly for first-home buyers.
  • The RBA's May decision was driven by persistent inflation above 3%, with staff projecting it won't return to the midpoint of the target band until mid-2028.
  • Despite rising rates nationally, the Sunshine Coast remains largely insulated, with equity-rich buyers — downsizers, upgraders and those relocating from more expensive cities — continuing to underpin local demand.

September Wrap 2026 • Bli Bli Market Update
A quiet quarter, a fresh rate rise, and a very different picture to the one before it. July to September was the quietest quarter Bli Bli has seen in at least two years, with 25 sales against 62 in the same quarter last year. Prices held firm, though: the 12 month median house price finished September at $1.155m, up about 6% on a year ago. The question heading into spring is whether that holds now that volume has thinned and the RBA has lifted the cash rate again. Here's the quarterly wrap, plus where the local and national markets sit.

Market pulse
  • Volume is the story. 25 sales is down about 60% on the 62 from Jul-Sep 2025, 44% below 2024, and 32% below the 37 in the June quarter. It's the lowest quarter in our two-year data, and the second quarter running below the 40 to 62 range we'd been used to.
  • Prices are holding. The 12 month median house price finished September at $1.155m, essentially flat on June's $1.157m and up about 6% on $1.087m a year ago. Sales volume has dropped, but values haven't followed it.
  • Rates are testing the buyer pool. At Bli Bli's $1m+ price points, each hike changes borrowing capacity. That fits with buyers taking longer to decide and fewer deals getting across the line.
National snapshot
  • The rate rise landed. The RBA lifted the cash rate 25 basis points to 4.60% on 29 September, a unanimous decision, the fourth hike of 2026 and the highest rate since November 2011. That's the September hike NAB was tipping last month. ANZ and Westpac are now both forecasting another in November (to 4.85%), with the RBA's next decision on 3 November and September-quarter CPI in late October likely to settle it.
  • Six months of falls. Cotality's national index fell 1.1% in September, the sixth straight monthly decline, leaving values 5.2% below the March 2026 peak. 97% of capital city suburbs recorded falls over the three months to September.
  • Brisbane is now leading the decline. Brisbane fell 1.5% in September, the sharpest drop among the capitals, and its sales volumes are down 27.2% on a year ago. That follows -0.6% in July and -1.0% in August, so the pullback is accelerating. It also mirrors our thin quarter and affects the Brisbane upgrader demand we flagged last month.

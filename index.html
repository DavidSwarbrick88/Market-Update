Market pulse
  • Bli Bli's median house price closed FY25-26 at $1,150,100, up 15% on FY24-25's $1,000,000 — steady growth across the year, not one hot quarter.
  • The June quarter came in under 30 sales, a clear dip from the 40+ every other quarter posted over the past two years — worth watching, though prices held up regardless.
  • Land sales nearly doubled (20 vs 10), driven by ongoing releases at Camelot Court and Camp Flat Road.
National snapshot
  • The national market has stalled — Cotality's Home Value Index fell 0.4% in June, though values are still 7.3% higher annually. Sydney and Melbourne are now in outright decline.
  • Brisbane and the Sunshine Coast are still rising, but losing pace — quarterly growth down to 1.3% from 3.4%.
  • The RBA held rates at 4.35% in June; the next call is 11 August, with the 29 July CPI print likely to decide a fourth hike.

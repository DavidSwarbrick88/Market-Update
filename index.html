Market pulse
  • The broader Sunshine Coast median now sits around $1.26m, up roughly 13.6% annually - 90 of the region's 95 suburbs are above the $1m mark, up from fewer than 20 just five years ago.
  • The year's top Bli Bli sale was $2.5m for a six-bedroom home on Cook Road in November - the top end of the market stayed active all year, not just the $1m-range family homes.
  • Four-bedroom homes were the busiest bracket in Bli Bli this year - 89 sales at a $1.195m median, well ahead of three-bedroom activity (59 sales, $1.02m median).
National snapshot
  • The national downturn widened, not stalled - Cotality's Home Value Index fell 0.7% in July, the steepest monthly drop since December 2022, led by Sydney (-1.4%) and Melbourne (-1.2%).
  • Brisbane's run has turned too - after 17.4% annual growth, the city posted its first real monthly decline (-0.6%) in July, with stock on market up 23.2% year-on-year giving buyers more choice.
  • Inflation came in softer than expected on 30 July (3.8%, down from 4.0%), and all four major banks now expect the RBA to hold at 4.35% on 11 August rather than hike.

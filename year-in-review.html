Market pulse

A strong year overall, with a soft finish

Bli Bli’s median house price climbed from $1m in FY24-25 to $1.15m in FY25-26 — a 15% lift across the year, built on steady, broad-based demand rather than a single hot quarter.

The June quarter cooled — worth watching, not worrying about.

Every quarter over the past two years has landed between 40 and 62 sales. The June 2026 quarter came in under 30 — a clear step down. Prices held up regardless (the highest quarterly median of the year, in fact), so this reads as fewer transactions rather than softer values.

Land selling briskly on the back of two estates

Land sales nearly doubled year-on-year — 20 in FY25-26 versus 10 in FY24-25 — almost entirely driven by ongoing releases at Camelot Court and Camp Flat Road. Land median held steady around $680k–$685k across both years.

National snapshot

  • The national market has stalled. Cotality’s Home Value Index fell 0.4% in June — the steepest monthly drop in three and a half years — though values are still 7.3% higher than a year ago. Sydney and Melbourne are leading the pullback, now 2.1% and 2.9% below their November 2025 peaks.
  • Brisbane (and the Sunshine Coast) are still rising, just more slowly. Brisbane’s median dwelling value sits around $1.12m, up 17.4% annually, but quarterly growth has eased to 1.3% from 3.4% the quarter before — the same loss of momentum showing up in Bli Bli’s sales volumes.
  • Rates on hold, for now. The RBA held the cash rate at 4.35% in June. The next call is 11 August, with the 29 July CPI print likely to decide whether a fourth hike is on the table.
  • Renters are feeling it more than owners. National vacancy rates sit at a record-low 1.5% and rents are up 5.9% annually — even as sale prices cool, the shortage of rental stock isn’t easing, which continues to support investor yields.
  • Investor tax changes are adding uncertainty. Proposed federal budget changes to negative gearing and capital gains tax concessions are expected to be legislated, likely to cool established-market investor activity further over the second half of 2026.
